Breathwatch

Stay with a breathing check. Nothing leaves this phone.

Breathwatch is not a medical device. It cannot call for help, text anyone, or contact emergency services on its own.

Keep the app open in the foreground with the screen on. If the screen sleeps or you leave this page, the check stops.

Never use alone. Call Never Use Alone at 1-800-484-3731 so a person can stay on the phone with you.

A soft chime, then a loud alarm, plays on this phone if breathing slows or a check-in goes unanswered. Sound is processed in memory only. Nothing is saved or sent.

Tap mode counts breaths you tap. Mic mode listens only while you leave it on, then releases the microphone.

Naloxone nasal spray

  1. Call 911.
  2. Give one spray of naloxone in one nostril.
  3. Give rescue breaths if they are not breathing.
  4. If there is no response after 2–3 minutes, give a second dose.

Stay with them. These are general public steps, not personal medical advice.

Recovery position

  1. Kneel beside them.
  2. Put the near arm at a right angle, palm up.
  3. Bring the far knee up and roll them toward you onto their side.
  4. Tilt the head back so the mouth points toward the floor and the airway stays open.
  5. Stay and watch breathing until help takes over.
